Fixed

  • Settings… did nothing in menu-bar-only mode. When LidBoot lives only in the menu bar it's usually not the active app when you click its popover, and in that state macOS's SettingsLink silently fails. Settings now opens — and comes to the front — from the popover, and from lidboot://settings.
  • Windows opened from the popover or a URL now reliably come to the front instead of appearing behind whatever you were using.
  • Switching Light/Dark/System now updates the Settings window you're looking at, not just the other windows.

New

  • lidboot://settings.
